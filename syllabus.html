Syllabus Architecture & Exam Strategy

Engineering Mathematics-I is a foundational 4-credit course for first-year B.Tech engineering branches at IKG-PTU (including CGC CEC Landran). The 75-mark theory exam consists of three distinct sections designed to test conceptual definitions, computational rigor, and extended mathematical derivations.

SEC A

Section A: Short Answer

10 Questions × 2 Marks = 20 Marks (Compulsory)

Covers all 5 units equally (exactly 2 questions per unit). Focuses on standard test statements (Ratio, Cauchy, Leibnitz, p-test), limit evaluation of sequences, Beta-Gamma evaluations ($\Gamma(1/2)$), Euler's theorem orders, and basic double integral limits.

SEC B

Section B: Medium Derivations

5 Questions × 5 Marks = 25 Marks (Choose 5 of 7)

Tests standard applications: arc length of cycloid/cardioid, surface area of sphere/ellipse, Beta-Gamma reduction integrals, Euler's 1st order verification, and 2D area by double integration.

SEC C

Section C: Long Comprehensive Proofs

3 Questions × 10 Marks = 30 Marks (Choose 3 of 5)

Full proofs and multi-stage evaluations: Proof of Euler's 2nd order theorem with deductions, Change of Order of Integration with diagram, Maxima/Minima of 2-variable functions (rectangular box), and Volume of solid of revolution.

Unit-Wise Syllabus Breakdown & Topic Coverage

Unit 1 Sequences & Infinite Series (4 Vault Topics)

Convergence of sequences and series, tests for positive term series: Comparison test, D'Alembert's ratio test, Raabe's test, Cauchy root test, Cauchy integral test, alternating series, Leibnitz's test, absolute & conditional convergence, Taylor's & Maclaurin's series.

Unit 2 Applications of Definite Integrals (8 Vault Topics)

Evaluation of definite and improper integrals, applications of definite integrals to evaluate surface areas and volumes of revolutions for Cartesian, parametric, and polar curves (Cycloid, Ellipse, Cardioid, Astroid).

Explore Unit 2
Unit 3 Beta & Gamma Functions & Improper Integrals (5 Vault Topics)

Beta and Gamma functions and their properties, relation between Beta and Gamma functions, evaluation of standard trigonometric integrals, duplication formula, convergence of improper integrals.

Explore Unit 3
Unit 4 Multivariable Calculus (Partial Differentiation) (5 Vault Topics)

Functions of several variables, limits, continuity, partial derivatives, total derivative, chain rule, Euler's theorem for homogeneous functions and its second-order deductions, maxima & minima of two variables, Lagrange's multipliers.

Explore Unit 4
Unit 5 Multiple Integrals & Applications (6 Vault Topics)

Double and triple integrals (Cartesian and Polar), change of order of integration, change of variables (Cartesian to Polar coordinates via Jacobians), applications of double & triple integrals to calculate area and volume.

Top 5 PTU Evaluator Traps (Where Students Lose Marks)

  • 1. Ratio Test Boundary ($l=1$): Never conclude convergence or divergence at $l=1$ without immediately transitioning to Raabe's test or logarithmic test. Writing just 'fails' without next step costs 2 marks.
  • 2. Cycloid Arc Length Limits: For one complete arch of a cycloid ($x = a(\theta - \sin\theta), y = a(1-\cos\theta)$), the parametric range is $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$, not $[0, \pi]$.
  • 3. Euler's 2nd Order Second Deduction: Remember that $x^2 u_{xx} + 2xy u_{xy} + y^2 u_{yy} = F(u)[F'(u) - 1]$ when the function is defined implicitly as $f(u) = \phi(x,y)$.
  • 4. Change of Order of Integration: PTU evaluators strictly check for the sketch of the region of integration. Solving the integral algebraically without drawing the region and showing horizontal/vertical strips loses 50% credit.
  • 5. Jacobian in Polar Coordinates: Always write $dx\,dy = |J|\,dr\,d\theta = r\,dr\,d\theta$. Forgetting the factor of $r$ leads to dimensionally inconsistent integrals.
