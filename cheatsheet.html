Unit 1: Convergence Tests

• Ratio Test: $\lim \frac{u_n}{u_{n+1}} = l$. ($l>1$ conv, $l<1$ div, $l=1$ fails).
• Raabe's Test: $\lim n\left(\frac{u_n}{u_{n+1}}-1\right) = l$.
• p-Series: $\sum \frac{1}{n^p}$ conv if $p>1$, div if $p \le 1$.

Unit 2: Lengths & Surfaces

• Arc Length: $s = \int \sqrt{1 + (y')^2}\,dx$ or $\int \sqrt{r^2 + (r')^2}\,d\theta$.
• Surface Area: $S = 2\pi \int y\,ds$ (about x-axis).
• Cardioid $r = a(1+\cos\theta)$: Perimeter = $8a$, Area = $\frac{3\pi a^2}{2}$.

Unit 3: Beta-Gamma Relations

• $B(m,n) = \frac{\Gamma(m)\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)}$.
• $\Gamma(1/2) = \sqrt{\pi}$, $\Gamma(n+1) = n\Gamma(n) = n!$.
• Reflection: $\Gamma(p)\Gamma(1-p) = \frac{\pi}{\sin(p\pi)}$.
• $\int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^p\theta \cos^q\theta\,d\theta = \frac{1}{2} B\left(\frac{p+1}{2}, \frac{q+1}{2}\right)$.

Unit 4: Euler & Extrema

• Euler's 1st: $x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = nu$.
• Euler's 2nd: $x^2 u_{xx} + 2xy u_{xy} + y^2 u_{yy} = n(n-1)u$.
• Extrema: $rt - s^2 > 0$ and $r>0$ (min), $r<0$ (max). Fails if $rt - s^2 < 0$ (saddle).

Unit 5: Multiple Integrals

• Polar Transform: $dx\,dy = r\,dr\,d\theta$.
• Jacobian: $J = \frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)} = \begin{vmatrix} x_u & x_v \\ y_u & y_v \end{vmatrix}$.
• Area: $\iint_R dx\,dy = \iint_R r\,dr\,d\theta$.
